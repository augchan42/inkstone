# Starter rubrics

Copy one into `rubric.md` and change it to fit the work. Each totals 10. A blind read that
names a different subject is blocking in every rubric.

## Still image, render or scene

| Category | Max | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Subject reads | 4 | A stranger names the brief's subject from the blind read, at the viewing size |
| Composition | 2 | The subject is large and placed where the eye lands; nothing competes with it |
| Light and value | 2 | The subject separates from its ground; nothing important lost in black or blown to white |
| Detail and finish | 2 | No broken edges, stray marks, artefacts or clipping |

## Glyph or low-resolution art (ASCII, canvas sampled to cells)

| Category | Max | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Subject reads | 4 | Named correctly in the blind read at the real cell grid, desktop and phone |
| Silhouette | 2 | The subject's outline is unmistakable; thin parts survive the cell size |
| Separation | 2 | Subject against ground by value, not by fine texture |
| Motion (if animated, judged from frames) | 2 | The movement is legible and loops without a jump |

## Video frames and contact sheets

| Category | Max | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Each beat reads | 4 | Every frame shows its beat's action without the narration |
| Continuity | 2 | Characters, props and light stay consistent between frames |
| Composition at phone size | 2 | Essential subject and text survive the 9:16 frame and the platform overlays |
| Finish | 2 | No artefacts, clipping or half-drawn states |

## Cover or thumbnail

| Category | Max | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Reads at feed size | 4 | Title and one key visual readable at about 180 x 320 |
| One idea | 2 | A single subject; no diagram shrunk to mush |
| Crop | 2 | Survives the profile-grid crop; nothing sliced at an edge |
| Finish | 2 | Clean type, even margins, no stray UI |

## Copy (captions, short text)

| Category | Max | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Says what it means | 4 | The blind read states the brief's point |
| Plain | 2 | Literal words; no filler or mannered phrasing |
| Fits the voice | 2 | Matches the account's or project's stated voice |
| Correct | 2 | Facts, names and spelling right |
